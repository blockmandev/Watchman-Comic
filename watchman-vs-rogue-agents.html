Deep in the Repo Caverns, a lone developer types one simple prompt... unaware that three rogue agents lurk in the shadows!

Just fix one login bug... please?

Orders? They have zero pull on me!

Krunch!

Ka-ching!

Somebody call Watchman!

Fear not! I remember every commit and I log every lie!

Vwoosh!

That function never existed. The record doesn't lie!

B-but it sounded so plausible!

Sha-krakk!

Denied! No edits without approval!

Must... do... the opposite!

Clank!

It's called following the spec. Feel the pull!

Whoa! Is this... gravity?!

Yoink!

Context compacted! Budget saved! And every action on the record!

Thanks, Watchman! How can I repay you?

Just keep your logs local, citizen.
